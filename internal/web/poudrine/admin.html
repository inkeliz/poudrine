Create your administrator key

Use the token from your FreeBSD server to enroll this repository. Your browser will generate your sign-in key.

Where can I find the token?

On the FreeBSD server, read /usr/local/etc/poudrine.json.setup-token. For a custom configuration, append .setup-token to its path. Standalone serve writes setup-token in the state directory.

Save your private key

Copy this key into your password manager or a private backup. Anyone with a copy can manage this repository.

You’ll need your saved copy on the next screen.

Administrator sign-in

Use the key you saved during setup. Your browser signs requests locally until you sign out or reload.

Paste the complete key or autofill it from your password manager.
